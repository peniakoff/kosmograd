Kosmograd: Sputnik

A space-program logistics chain for Workers & Resources: Soviet Republic.

This is chapter one of Kosmograd: fabricate Zarya-K1, haul it on a dedicated rail wagon, fuel the pad, and launch. Nothing teleports. University-educated workers are required. Rail curves must be gentle — the transporter-erector is long.

Current workshop build may be grey-box (correct footprints, placeholder art). That is intentional.

Requires a late-game industrial base: steel, aluminium, mechanical and electronic components, oil, chemicals, and a stable power grid.

This mod does not edit vanilla files. It does not add a new resource type. Rockets and satellites are vehicles.

Not affiliated with any historical design bureau. Names are original (Zarya, Vestnik). The subtitle "Sputnik" refers to the era of the first satellite, not a replica of a specific spacecraft.
