Kosmograd: Sputnik

A space-program logistics chain for Workers & Resources: Soviet Republic.

PROTOTYPE CATALOG — NOT INSTALLABLE YET.

The target for chapter one is to fabricate Zarya-K1 from delivered vanilla resources, haul it on a dedicated rail wagon, fuel the pad if the engine test succeeds, and complete the verified rocket lifecycle. University-educated workers are required. Rail curves must follow the geometry proven during testing.

No public Workshop build exists yet. Engine behavior, object packaging and art are still in P0 feasibility work.

The P1 target requires a late-game industrial base: steel, aluminium, mechanical and electronic components, university workers and a stable power grid. Fuel is listed as a requirement only after S5 passes.

This mod does not edit vanilla files. It does not add a new resource type. Rockets and satellites are vehicles.

Not affiliated with any historical design bureau. Names are original (Zarya, Vestnik). The subtitle "Sputnik" refers to the era of the first satellite, not a replica of a specific spacecraft.
